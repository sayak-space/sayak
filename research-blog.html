Exploring the science behind my research

This section is where I try to explain some of the ideas that lie behind my research in a simple and accessible way. From the lower ionosphere and VLF radio waves to solar flares and the solar cycle, these notes are intended for anyone curious about the physics of the Sun–Earth system.

Starting Point

A Journey from the Sun to the Ionosphere

The Sun is not simply a distant source of light and heat. Its changing activity continuously influences the near-Earth environment. Solar radiation can alter the ionosphere, which in turn affects radio-wave propagation around our planet. Understanding this chain of interactions is at the heart of much of my research.

What is the Solar Cycle?

Solar activity changes over time in a roughly eleven-year cycle. Sunspots, flares and other indicators of solar activity rise and fall as the Sun moves between solar minimum and solar maximum.

How Can VLF Signals Probe the Ionosphere?

Very Low Frequency radio signals travel over enormous distances by interacting with the Earth–ionosphere waveguide. This makes them useful tools for remotely sensing the lower ionosphere.
