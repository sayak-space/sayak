23rd National Space Science Symposium (NSSS 2026)

23–27 February 2026 · North Eastern Space Applications Centre (NESAC), Meghalaya
Organised by North Eastern Space Applications Centre (NESAC) and Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
Poster Presentation

My Contribution

“An Integrated Framework for Modeling Daytime and Seasonal Variability in VLF Signal Propagation”

I presented an integrated framework combining D-region electron continuity modelling, Wait’s ionospheric model and LWPC to simulate sub-ionospheric VLF signal propagation. The model was validated against multi-station VLF observations from ICSP and used to study the temporal and spatial behaviour of VLF signals during summer and winter conditions.

2025

3rd Young Astronomers of Today's India (YATI3)

7–9 October 2025 · Indian Centre for Space Physics (ICSP), Kolkata
Oral Presentation

My Contribution

“Integrated Modeling of D-region Ionospheric Response: Application to Solar Flare Events and Quiet-Time VLF Diurnal Profiles”

I presented an overview of my research on D-region ionospheric responses to solar forcing and their signatures in sub-ionospheric VLF signals. The presentation covered an integrated modelling framework connecting solar X-ray/EUV irradiance, electron density, Wait’s ionospheric parameters and LWPC simulations.

The framework was applied to both solar-flare events and quiet-time summer–winter VLF observations, demonstrating its ability to reproduce observed VLF signal behaviour under different solar ionizing conditions.

2024

URSI-Regional Conference in Radio Science (RCRS 2024)

22–25 October 2024 · Graphic Era Hill University, Bhimtal, Uttarakhand
Organised by the Indian Radio Science Society (InRaSS)
Oral Presentation

My Contribution

“Modelling of sub-ionospheric VLF signal propagation during a solar flare: an autonomous approach”

I presented an autonomous modelling framework connecting solar X-ray irradiance with sub-ionospheric VLF signal propagation. The framework combines an electron continuity equation model, extraction of Wait’s ionospheric reflection parameters and LWPC simulations.

Using the C4.4 solar flare of 28 March 2017 as a case study, the simulated VLF amplitude showed close agreement with observations, demonstrating the potential of the model for predicting VLF behaviour during solar flares.

2024

22nd National Space Science Symposium (NSSS 2024)

26 February – 1 March 2024 · Goa University, Goa
Organised by Goa University and Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
Oral Presentation

My Contribution

“On the Laggy Nature of D-region Ionosphere during Solar Flares”

I presented my work on the delayed response of the lower ionosphere to enhanced solar X-ray radiation during solar flares. Using numerical solutions of the D-region electron continuity equation and GOES X-ray observations, I investigated the temporal evolution of electron density and the associated response-time delay.

The study examined the dependence of the response time on solar zenith angle, flare intensity, altitude, latitude and season. The results showed significant altitude and solar-zenith-angle dependence of the D-region response, while the response time exhibited an inverse relationship with solar-flare energy.
