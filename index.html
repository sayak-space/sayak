About Me

I am currently a Senior Project Associate (Postdoctoral Fellow) at the Aditya-L1 Support Cell of the Aryabhatta Research Institute of Observational Sciences (ARIES), Nainital, India.

I completed my PhD research in theoretical physics at the University of Calcutta, with research carried out at the Indian Centre for Space Physics (ICSP), Kolkata.

My research has primarily focused on understanding the response of the lower ionosphere to solar energetic events using numerical modelling and Very Low Frequency radio signal observations.

Research Interests

D-region Ionosphere

Numerical modelling and investigation of the lower ionospheric D-region and its response to solar radiation.

VLF Remote Sensing

Study of sub-ionospheric Very Low Frequency radio signal propagation as a remote sensing technique for the lower ionosphere.

Solar Flares

Investigation of ionospheric electron density variations and response times associated with solar flares.

Solar–Terrestrial Physics

Exploring the coupling between solar activity and Earth's ionosphere using observations and numerical modelling.
